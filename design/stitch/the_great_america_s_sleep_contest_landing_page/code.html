Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about America's quietest competition.

What happens if I wake up during the 90 minutes? expand_more
If your heart rate exceeds baseline threshold for more than 10 seconds or you open your eyes and sit up, your biometric status flips from "Deep Slumber" to "Awake." You may quietly step into the Sleep Lounge to enjoy breakfast waffles and watch the rest of the arena compete on the big screens.
How is the $100,000 winner judged? expand_more
All 200,000 contestants wear non-invasive biometric telemetry patches that monitor heart rate variability (HRV), pulse steadiness, and delta-wave sleep scores. The contestant with the lowest, most undisturbed cardiac and motion profile through all three wake-up squad rounds takes 1st place.
Can I bring my own pillow and blanket? expand_more
Yes! You are welcome to bring one personal standard-size pillow and one blanket. We also supply a standardized premium cloud-foam sleep mat and contest-issued eye masks. However, noise-canceling earplugs and sleeping pills are strictly forbidden.
Where and when will the contest take place? expand_more
The national event will be hosted at a major enclosed NFL domed stadium once 200,000 registrations are met. The final stadium location and exact date will be locked in with 90 days notice, allowing all participants ample travel booking time. If you cannot attend, your $10 deposit is 100% refundable upon request.
What if I need to use the restroom during the 90 minutes? expand_more
A 45-minute staging and preparation window occurs directly before the official countdown starts. Once the 90-minute sleep clock initiates, leaving your mat constitutes voluntary forfeit from the grand prize competition. Plan your hydration accordingly!
